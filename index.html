Work

Two things we built.

Clinical decision support

A tumour board assistant

For a precision oncology company

A molecular tumour board has to read a patient’s clinical history and molecular results together, then turn that reading into paperwork. We built an agentic research tool that does the gathering and the drafting.

It is used in two ways. A tumour board uses it to support a second-opinion service. Clinicians use it to interpret clinical and molecular data side by side.

Clinical decision support and research tools

Drafts
Reports, referral letters and compassionate access letters
Aim
Great cancer care for more patients, at lower cost
Commercial
$500k annual recurring revenue

Preclinical

Paediatric fracture detection

For a paediatric imaging company

Children’s bones are not small adult bones. Growth plates and developing anatomy change what a fracture looks like. We built a radiology model that detects extremity fractures in patients from birth to 21.

It is being developed as AI as a medical device.
